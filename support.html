How can we help?

Characterize does not require an account. For help with importing data or running a simulation, try the steps below or send us a message.

Quick checks

  • Add a built-in structure from Library before opening the XRD, SAED or EBSD workspace.
  • For CIF imports, confirm the file contains valid unit-cell parameters and atomic-site data.
  • COD Search and Fetch require an internet connection and availability of crystallography.net.
  • Include the app version, device model and steps to reproduce when reporting a problem.

Contact

Email is the fastest way to reach Characterize support. Please do not include confidential experimental data unless it is needed to explain the issue.
