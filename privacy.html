Characterize Support Privacy

Effective November 4, 2026

Privacy policy

Characterize is designed to keep crystal-structure editing and scientific simulations on your device.

Local processing

Characterize edits crystal structures and calculates diffraction simulations on your device. Files you select are read locally. The app does not upload imported CIF files, experimental patterns or your workspace to a developer-operated server. Exports are saved or shared only through actions you initiate.

The app does not include an account system, advertising SDK or analytics SDK, and does not perform advertising tracking.

Optional online database requests

When you choose Search or Fetch in the COD feature, Characterize sends selected element filters or a COD identifier to the Crystallography Open Database at crystallography.net over HTTPS. These requests do not upload your local workspace or imported files.

Characterize uses an ephemeral network session, disables cookie acceptance and avoids its URL response cache. These settings do not control COD's server logs or retention.

COD states that server logs may include IP addresses, access timestamps and user-agent information for technical operations, security and visitor statistics. See the COD privacy policy for details.

Support communications

If you contact support, information you choose to provide, including your email and message, is used to respond to your request. Do not send confidential experimental data unless needed for support.
